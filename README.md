# 🎬 My Favorite Series

## 1. Stranger Things
**Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Drama  
**Created by:** The Duffer Brothers  

> *"Friends don't lie."*  
A group of kids in the 1980s discover strange events in their small town after a young boy disappears. Mysterious experiments, supernatural creatures, and a girl with powers named **Eleven** make this show thrilling and nostalgic.  

**Main Characters:**
- Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers  

---

## 2. The 100
**Genre:** Post-Apocalyptic, Sci-Fi, Drama  
**Based on:** The book series by Kass Morgan  

> *"Maybe there are no good guys."*  
After a nuclear apocalypse, survivors in space send **100 juvenile delinquents** back to Earth to see if it’s habitable again. The series explores leadership, survival, and human nature.

**Main Characters:**
- Clarke Griffin
- Bellamy Blake
- Octavia Blake
- Raven Reyes  

## 3. Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)
**Genre:** Crime, Thriller, Drama  
**Created by:** Álex Pina  

> *"The most important moments are the ones that make you realize there’s no turning back."*  
A mastermind called **The Professor** recruits eight criminals to carry out the biggest heist in Spain — robbing the **Royal Mint**. The show is full of suspense, strategy, and emotion.  

**Main Characters:**
- The Professor
- Tokyo
- Berlin
- Nairobi
- Rio  

### 💭 Why I Love These Series
All three shows have:
- Strong characters 💪  
- Emotional storylines ❤️  
- Intense suspense 😱
